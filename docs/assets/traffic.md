# Traffic vehicles (agent "traffic")

**Licence: project-owned.** All eight vehicles (sedan, hatch, suv, sports, van, boxtruck, semi, tanker) are our own
procedural work, generated by Python scripts in headless Blender 4.3. No third-party models, textures, logos,
trademarks or brand names; plates use abstract glyphs, and the tanker's hazard diamond and orange panels are generic and
carry no numbers.

## How they are built
- `tools/traffic/vlib.py` holds the mesh helpers (loft, lathe, box, wheel, AO bake, glTF export) and the material palette (colour / roughness / metalness per surface class).
- `tools/traffic/build.py` builds the cars (lofted bodies, decal grids for lamps, seams and plates) and calls `trucks.py`.
- `tools/traffic/trucks.py` builds the trucks: a cab-over tractor (windscreen, sun visor, roof fairing on the semi, mirrors, steps, cab extenders, fuel tank, exhaust stack), chassis rails, a fifth wheel, axles with dual rear wheels, mud flaps, landing gear, side guards, an underride bar with red/white reflective strips, red/yellow rear chevron plates, tail-lamp clusters and side marker lamps. The box body and box trailer have door ribs or a roller shutter, lock rods, corner castings and conspicuity tape. The tanker has a polished tank with domed ends, stiffening bands, a catwalk, manlids, handrails and a ladder.
- Every vertex gets baked ambient occlusion (ray-cast, with a ground plane) in its colour channel.
- `tools/traffic/pack.mjs` merges each Blender export into one primitive per type: POSITION, NORMAL, COLOR_0 (albedo × AO × road grime) and `_SURF` (surface class, roughness, metalness). Paint, lamps, retro-reflectors and glass are told apart in the shader by `_SURF`. The same script:
  - simplifies each type to its triangle budget with meshoptimizer (attribute-aware, edges between surface classes stay watertight),
  - writes a distance LOD `<type>_lod1`. Its lamps are locked, then enlarged about 2.3× so they still read at 150 m. The factory switches to it beyond 120 m,
  - compresses the file with meshopt into `public/assets/models/traffic/traffic.glb`.
- At runtime `src/traffic/vehicleFactory.ts` gives each vehicle one MeshStandardMaterial and a patched shader. No textures, no skinning.

## Rebuild
```
pwsh tools/traffic/blender.ps1 all           # or e.g. semi,boxtruck,tanker   (Idle priority; out: assets-src/traffic/out)
node tools/traffic/pack.mjs                  # -> public/assets/models/traffic/traffic.glb
node tools/traffic/tricount.mjs              # per-material triangle counts of the Blender exports
```
To review: `sandbox/traffic.html?car=0&type=<t>&view=rear|front|side|rq|fq|far|farf&brake=1&ind=1&charred=1&dist=<m>`,
plus `tools/traffic/sheet.mjs` for contact sheets. In the game: `tools/scripts/traffic-trucks.mjs` (shots) and `tools/scripts/traffic-cost.mjs` (exact draw and triangle count per frame).
